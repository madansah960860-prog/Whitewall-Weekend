Contact Whitewall Weekend

By the Whitewall Weekend Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

We read every message ourselves, and nothing cheers us like a note from a fellow car lover. The best way to reach us is by email — there is no form to fill in and no account to create.

Write to us

Send your note to garage@whitewallweekend.com. Tell us which article you were reading and what is on your mind, and we will do our best to help. We aim to reply within two business days.

Spotted a date or a detail you believe is wrong? Our editors welcome corrections at editor@whitewallweekend.com. We check our history carefully, but we are human, and we will gladly put right any genuine error.

What we can and cannot help with

  • We can share general history, point you to the right article, or fix a mistake on a page.
  • We cannot value your car, diagnose a mechanical fault from afar, or recommend specific shops — a local club or specialist is far better placed for that.
  • We offer no paid services and will never ask you for payment.

A few common questions

Is Whitewall Weekend free?
Entirely. Every article is free to read, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments and run no advertising.
Can you tell me what my classic is worth?
We cannot offer appraisals. A local club, a specialist, or a recognized value guide for your make and model is the right source.
Do I need to own a classic to enjoy the hobby?
Not at all. Many of the happiest people at any show arrived in an ordinary car and spent the day admiring and chatting. Enthusiasm is the only requirement.
Will you add more articles?
Yes, over time, and reader suggestions often shape what we cover next. Tell us which cars and topics you would like to see.
