Whitewall Weekend is written for the love of classic cars, as a hobby and a bit of history — not as a repair manual or a professional appraisal. Our maintenance notes are general and cover only basic, low-risk care; for anything mechanical beyond that, and for any work involving brakes, fuel, or the underside of a raised vehicle, please consult a qualified mechanic.
